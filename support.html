Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → ZhanZhuang.

How does the free trial work?

ZhanZhuang offers 3 free sessions with full access. You will not be charged unless you decide to purchase the subscription afterwards. You can cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

I was charged after cancelling. What do I do?

All billing is handled by Apple. Please contact Apple Support at support.apple.com for billing issues.
